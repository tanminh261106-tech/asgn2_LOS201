Assignment 02 - SMS Sensor Monitoring System
Real-Time Jitter Comparison

Test configuration:
- Samples per policy: 1000
- Period: 1 ms
- Clock: CLOCK_MONOTONIC
- Sleep method: clock_nanosleep with TIMER_ABSTIME
- Memory locking: mlockall(MCL_CURRENT | MCL_FUTURE)

SCHED_OTHER:
- Minimum jitter: 52906 ns
- Maximum jitter: 990504 ns
- Average jitter: 70758.65 ns
- CSV rows including header: 1001

SCHED_FIFO:
- Priority: 50
- Minimum jitter: 2565 ns
- Maximum jitter: 99551 ns
- Average jitter: 16222.84 ns
- CSV rows including header: 1001

Comparison:
In this test run, SCHED_FIFO had lower minimum, maximum,
and average jitter than SCHED_OTHER.
Average jitter was approximately 77.1% lower with SCHED_FIFO.

Limitations:
Measurements were performed on the Ubuntu host.
They are not measurements from the QEMU target.
Results can vary with system load and scheduling conditions.
